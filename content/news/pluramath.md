We released **[PluraMath](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05992)**, a human-curated benchmark extending mathematical reasoning evaluation to 18 underrepresented languages across six language families.

The work evaluates 27 reasoning LLMs and finds a persistent performance gap between high-resource and underrepresented languages. I contributed as a co-author and Serbian-language contributor.

[Read the paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05992) · [Explore the code](https://github.com/TUM-NLP/pluramath) · [View the dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/tum-nlp/PluraMath)

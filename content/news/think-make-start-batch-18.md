I had a great experience taking part in [THINK.MAKE.START.](https://www.tms.tum.de/) Batch #18, a two-week innovation program organized by TUM and UnternehmerTUM.

The program brought together interdisciplinary teams to develop and test new product ideas before presenting their prototypes on Demo Day. I met many smart and wonderful people working on innovative and fun projects throughout the two weeks.

Batch #18 concluded with [Demo Day on March 22, 2024](https://portal.mytum.de/termine/Event.2023-12-20.5969858050).

Many thanks to all the organizers and coaches who made the experience possible.
